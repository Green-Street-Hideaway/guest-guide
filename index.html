Local sights & excursions

From the mystical Hell Cave (Jama Pekel) to flower parks and medieval castles, the Savinja Valley is packed with hidden gems perfect for a morning trip or a full day out.

Explore day trip ideas
Explore on two wheels

Šempeter is the heart of Slovenia's hop-growing region. Enjoy a flat, relaxing ride through the iconic "Green Gold" hop fields or follow the scenic Savinja River. For those seeking a challenge, the surrounding hills offer rewarding climbs and panoramic views.
